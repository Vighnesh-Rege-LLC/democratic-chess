    Article II · Political friction

    Pineapple Party Anchovy AllianceEvery Citizen belongs to one. Both sides have both.
    Not my party

    When one of your Citizens is captured, your Citizens from the other party won't recapture on that square. Their move shows a red dashed ring.

    Conscientious objection

    Any Citizen move has a 1-in-8 chance of being refused. Your turn isn't lost. You just have to ask someone else.

    General strike

    Every six moves, a random Citizen walks off the job for three turns. Striking Citizens can't move, but they still glare.
